5.6a Android location service (geocoder) in the astronomy calculator

The astronomy calculator converts place information via the geocoder built into Android — in both directions: a typed place name becomes coordinates, and for entered coordinates the matching place name is determined and shown in the location field.

  • Data transmitted: the typed place name or the entered coordinate pair. The request is made by the Android system; which service answers it depends on the device (on devices with Google services these are Google's location services).
  • Trigger: exclusively your input in the astronomy calculator. The place-name lookup starts only ~0.7 seconds after the last change and only for valid coordinates.
  • Important: a coordinate pair entered by hand is not your location, but the place you want to calculate for. Your actual location is only transmitted if you tap the location icon yourself.
  • Purpose: entering the observing site conveniently and recognizing the entered numbers.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) lit. b GDPR (performing the function you requested).
  • Without a network connection, or on devices without a location service, this conversion is unavailable; the calculator remains fully usable with coordinates entered by hand.

6. ML Kit (text recognition, handwriting, barcode)

For text recognition (OCR), handwriting recognition, barcode scanning and entity recognition, Google ML Kit is used. The actual recognition runs entirely on your device (on-device); images and stroke data do not leave your device in the process.

When an ML Kit model is first used, Android downloads the respective model from Google servers to your device (standard HTTP request, IP address, app identifier). After that, recognition is possible offline. Details: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms

7. Transmission to third countries

Some of the providers mentioned above are based outside the EU:

  • USA (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google): Transmission takes place on the basis of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, insofar as the recipient is certified, or the EU standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 (2) lit. c GDPR).
  • China (DeepSeek, Qwen / Alibaba Cloud): There is no adequacy decision by the EU Commission for China. Transmission only takes place if you actively select this provider and enter an API key; it is based on your explicit consent (Art. 49 (1) lit. a GDPR) and, where offered by the provider, on the EU standard contractual clauses. A level of data protection equivalent to that of the EU cannot be guaranteed in this case.

Mistral AI (France) processes within the EU; there is no transfer to a third country in this respect.

You can prevent transmission by not using the respective function (AI recognition: do not enter an API key; weather: do not use the weather function).

8. Retention period

  • Local data on your device is stored until you delete it in the app, clear the app data in the Android settings or uninstall the app.
  • Weather requests are processed by the provider according to its own privacy statement; the app itself only stores the most recently retrieved result locally as a cache.
  • Crashlytics reports are typically retained for 90 days in accordance with Firebase requirements.
  • AI requests are stored by the respective provider in accordance with its privacy policy.
  • Purchase data is retained by Google Play according to its own rules; the app only stores the Pro status locally.

9. Security

  • All external network connections take place exclusively via HTTPS / TLS.
  • API keys for AI providers are stored encrypted using Android Keystore via androidx.security.crypto (AES-256).
  • The app does not make any automated individual decisions within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR and does not carry out any profiling.
